        Spin The Wheel

        A quick tour — about thirty seconds. You can bring this back any time with How it works in the top corner.

        1
        Put things on the wheel

        In the Segments tab, type a name and press Add. The number beside each one is how many slices it gets — four slices means four times the chance of winning.

        It starts with the preset list loaded, so you can spin straight away.

        2
        Name your draw

        Click the title at the very top and type over it — Match Day, for example. The name shows above the wheel when you present, on the winner pop-up, and on any image you save.

        3
        One wheel, or two

        One wheel draws a single thing. Two-stage draw spins twice — first for who wins, then for what they win — and announces "Alice wins the Prize".

        Each list has its own Add box, preset and Clear button.

        4
        Got a long list?

        The Import tab takes a copy-paste straight out of Excel or Google Sheets, or a CSV you drag onto it. Quantities can be written "Name x3", "Name, 3", or as a second column.

        5
        Make it behave how you want

        In Options: take each winner off the wheel as they're drawn (ideal for prize draws), change how long the spin lasts, and turn the ticking, confetti or duplicate-spreading on and off.

        6
        Keep the result

        When the wheel stops, Save image makes a tidy branded picture to drop into Google Chat, an email or a slide.

        The Results tab keeps every winner, and exports them to a spreadsheet.

        Running it live? Present or P hides the setup panel, Space spins, Esc comes back. Your setup is remembered on this computer for next time.
